The part that changes everything

A yellow leaf never has just one cause

Watered yesterday and turning yellow means too much water. Two weeks without water and turning yellow means exactly the opposite. Same symptom, opposite cause.

That is why Broto never gives a single verdict. It ranks the causes by likelihood and hands you the test that tells them apart — the thing a photo app usually does not do.

Reminders

A reminder that never becomes spam

A bar at the top tells you how many plants need water today. It is the only block of full colour in the whole app, and that is on purpose: you open it, look, and close it.

You choose the time, in your own timezone. No “tip of the day”, no notification invented to pull you back in.

Brotinho

Trust

What Broto does not do

No pesticide dosages

What you can do without buying anything comes first: prune the affected leaf, adjust watering, improve airflow, isolate it from the plant next to it. If a product is needed, it names the category and tells you to follow the label.

Never says a plant is edible

Never, not even if you insist. That is not the kind of mistake you can fix afterwards.

Never guesses the species

A close-up of a sick leaf sometimes does not show the plant. When that happens it says it does not know and asks for a photo of the whole plant.

Never charges for its own failure

If the model cannot read the photo, the credit comes straight back and the app says so on screen.

Does not replace a vet

The pet-toxicity warning shows up, always with the caveat that it is a warning, not a diagnosis.

Does not sell your data

We do not ask for precise location, we do not read your gallery — only the photo you choose to send.
